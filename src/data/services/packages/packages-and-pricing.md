# How much does a website cost?

- **Topic:** Packages and Pricing
- **Source ID:** packages-and-pricing
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/services

This document describes the Website Packages offering: a new website built from scratch. It covers the three packages and their starting prices. It does not cover the Go Support Plan, which is a separate service for a website or application that already exists and is priced on a different scale. A visitor asking about fixing, improving, or deploying an existing site needs the Go Support Plan, whose prices start at A$40 per hour for a consultation and A$100 for a standard engagement.

Nelson builds professional websites for small businesses, independent professionals, and growing brands. Every website is built to be responsive, maintainable, fast, and easy to manage, using modern development practices and production-ready deployment.

There are three website packages, each priced as a starting point from a fixed figure in Australian dollars:

The Starter package starts from A$1,000 and suits simple business websites and a professional online presence. It covers a small business website with up to five standard pages and no content management complexity.

The Business package starts from A$2,200 and suits businesses requiring a CMS, a blog, SEO configuration, and integrations. This is the recommended package. It is the typical choice for a small business that needs responsive website development, a customised user interface, a homepage, general information pages, a blog, a content management system, a contact form, email integration, technical SEO, analytics, and production deployment.

The Business+ package starts from A$3,500 and suits custom websites needing advanced functionality and integrations. It adds advanced custom UI, custom animations, multiple content types, and API integrations on top of the Business package.

All three packages include two revision rounds and production deployment to a live domain.

These three figures are build prices for a new website. They are not support rates, and they do not apply to work on an application that already exists. Support work on an existing site or application is priced under the Go Support Plan, which uses a consultation rate from A$40 per hour, a standard A$100 engagement, and a custom quote for migrations and major changes.

Final pricing is not fixed from the starting figure alone. It depends on the agreed project scope, the required functionality, the number of pages, the integrations, and the content requirements. A fixed-price scope and quotation are prepared before development begins, once the requirements are confirmed.

The Business package is the recommended option for a typical small-business website because a business site usually needs an editable blog and a content management system. Choose Starter when the site is a small online presence without content management, and choose Business+ when the project needs advanced functionality, custom integrations, or multiple content types.
