# What is included in the Business website package?

- **Topic:** Packages and Pricing
- **Source ID:** business-package
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/services

This document describes the Website Packages offering: a new website built from scratch. It covers the Business package only, and it does not cover the Go Support Plan, which is a separate service for a website or application that already exists and is priced on a different scale. A visitor asking for help with an existing site needs the Go Support Plan, not this package.

The Business package costs from A$2,200 and is the recommended package. It is a complete business website with content management, blog functionality, SEO configuration, analytics, and common business integrations.

Everything in the Starter package is included in the Business package, which is priced from A$1,000 and covers up to 5 standard pages: responsive desktop, tablet and mobile development, a homepage, an about page, services or information pages, a contact page, a contact form, basic technical SEO, metadata configuration, a sitemap, robots.txt, production deployment, domain configuration assistance, responsive and browser testing, and two revision rounds.

On top of that foundation, the Business package adds:

- Customised user interface
- Headless CMS integration
- Blog system
- Blog listing page
- Individual article pages
- CMS content modelling
- Contact and transactional email integration
- Basic structured data
- Enhanced technical SEO
- Analytics integration
- Social sharing metadata
- Content management interface
- Production environment configuration
- Two revision rounds

A standard Business package may include a homepage, an about page, a services page, a blog, individual blog article pages, a contact page, a privacy policy, and terms and conditions.

The Business package is suitable for established small businesses, professional service providers, businesses publishing regular content, companies requiring an easy-to-manage website, and marketing-focused websites. It is the typical choice for a small business that needs responsive website development, a customised user interface, a homepage, general information pages, a blog, a content management system, a contact form, email integration, technical SEO, analytics, and production deployment.

The A$2,200 figure is a starting price for building a new website. It is not a support rate, and it is not the fee for fixing, improving, or deploying an application that already exists.
