# What are foreign keys and database indexes, and why are they useful?

- **Category:** Fundamental Questions
- **Source ID:** foreign-keys-and-indexes
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/chat

Based on my understanding, a foreign key is like a link between records. I think of it similarly to clicking a link on a website, where the link points you to another webpage. In a database, the foreign key points to a related record. For indexes, I think of them like a table of contents or a navigation bar on a website. They help the database locate related records more quickly and efficiently instead of searching through everything.
