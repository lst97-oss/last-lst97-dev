# What was your exact contribution to your selected project?

- **Category:** Introduction and Background
- **Source ID:** exact-contribution
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/chat

I was responsible for most parts of the project, from the initial design and architecture through development, debugging, infrastructure setup, and production deployment. That included frontend and backend development, database design, authentication, third-party integrations, Docker configuration, CI/CD, networking, and deployment. One of the more challenging areas was infrastructure and CI/CD because the problems are not always directly related to application code. For example, I had to configure SSH access, Tailscale networking to communicate through NAT, Cloudflare Tunnel, Docker Compose, DNS, and deployment pipelines. I also had to design the infrastructure with cost in mind. Because the project did not require expensive enterprise infrastructure at its current scale, I tried to keep the deployment architecture affordable while still making it maintainable and capable of scaling later. This project gave me practical experience across the complete lifecycle of a production application rather than only working on one isolated part of the stack.
