# Any reason why after you graduated you did not have any job experience as a software engineer?

- **Category:** Introduction and Background
- **Source ID:** no-software-engineering-job-experience
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/chat

After graduating, I felt that what I learned at university gave me a good foundation, but I also understood that real-world software development is much more complex than academic projects. So during that period, I focused on building my own projects from the ground up using different technology stacks. I wanted to understand the entire development lifecycle — from defining requirements and designing the architecture, to building an MVP, working with databases and APIs, deployment, and eventually maintaining and improving the product. Working on my own projects also gave me the freedom to experiment, make mistakes, evaluate different technical approaches, and understand why certain architectural decisions work better than others. With AI changing the development process, I also realised that developers increasingly need to understand more than just writing code. We need to be able to evaluate solutions, integrate different technologies, understand system design, and turn an idea into a working product. That period was therefore a period of self-directed engineering practice rather than a break from engineering: I was building, deploying, and maintaining complete products across different stacks in order to understand the full lifecycle of real software, and my goal was to enter a professional team with a stronger foundation so that I could contribute more effectively while continuing to learn from experienced developers.
