# Where do you see your software-development career progressing over the next two years?

- **Category:** Introduction and Background
- **Source ID:** career-next-two-years
- **URL:** https://www.lst97.dev/chat

Over the next two years, I want to continue developing into a stronger full-stack software engineer and gain experience across a wider range of real production systems. In the short term, I want to work on different and technically interesting projects where I can combine modern technologies, including AI-assisted development, to solve problems efficiently. I am especially interested in improving my skills in system architecture, backend development, infrastructure, database optimisation, AI integration, and lower-level programming. Long term, one area I am very interested in is reverse engineering. I would like to eventually develop the skills required to analyse compiled software, understand how programs behave at a lower level, and work with areas such as binaries, operating systems, Assembly, and software internals. I also think the role of software engineers will continue to change significantly because of AI. AI will make implementation faster and allow developers to ship features much more frequently, but I do not think software engineering will simply become unnecessary. Developers will still need to communicate with customers and stakeholders, understand the actual business problem, translate requirements into a technical design, evaluate trade-offs, verify that systems behave correctly, and design software that remains maintainable as requirements change. Because implementation may become faster, I think developers will spend relatively more time on architecture, problem definition, validation, system integration, and communication with customers. My goal over the next two years is therefore not only to become faster at writing code, but to become better at understanding problems and designing complete, maintainable systems around them.
